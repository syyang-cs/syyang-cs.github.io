<section class="home-section bio-section lang-block lang-en active" lang="en">
  <h2>Biography</h2>
  <p>
    I am a Professor at the School of Cyberspace Security, Guangzhou University, and Director of the Big Data Computing and Intelligence Lab. My research focuses on data management and data-intensive systems for emerging applications, especially spatio-temporal data, graph data, scalable query processing, and hardware/system acceleration for big data computing.
  </p>
  <p>
    I am a Senior Member of CCF, a standing committee member of the CCF Information Systems Committee and the Intelligent Transportation Committee, and an executive committee member of the CCF Database Committee. I serve as a guest editor for <em>Data Science and Engineering</em>, a special issue editor for <em>Journal of Computer Research and Development</em>, and a program committee member or reviewer for leading conferences and journals including PVLDB, ICDE, and IEEE TKDE. My work has been supported by national, provincial, ministerial, and industrial collaborative projects, and has been deployed at Ant Group with an Outstanding Application Project Award.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="home-section bio-section lang-block lang-zh" lang="zh-Hans">
  <h2>个人简介</h2>
  <p>
    我是广州大学网络空间安全学院教授、大数据计算与智能研究所所长。主要从事面向新型应用的大数据管理与数据密集型系统研究，研究方向包括时空数据管理、图数据管理、可扩展查询处理，以及面向大数据计算的硬件与系统加速技术。
  </p>
  <p>
    我是 CCF 高级会员，担任 CCF 信息系统专委会常务委员、智慧交通分会常务委员、数据库专委会执行委员；同时担任 CCF 期刊 <em>Data Science and Engineering</em> 客座编辑、《计算机研究与发展》专题编委，并长期担任 PVLDB、ICDE、IEEE TKDE 等国际顶级期刊与会议的程序委员或审稿人。相关研究获得国家、省部级项目和企业合作项目支持，部分成果已在蚂蚁集团落地应用并获得优秀应用项目奖。
  </p>
</section>

    <p class="section-intro">My group studies efficient data management techniques from algorithms to systems, with emphasis on real-world, large-scale, and dynamic data.</p>

  </div>
